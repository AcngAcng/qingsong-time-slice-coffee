# Time Slice Coffee Hackathon Pitch Deck Outline

## Deck intent

An 8-slide hackathon pitch for Time Slice Coffee: an AI-powered time-slice information system that feels like a new information consumer product, not an AI SaaS dashboard.

Core one-liner:

> Time Slice Coffee 是一个把今天真实信息流中的重要变化，萃取成不同身份可理解、可带走的“时代信息切片咖啡”的产品原型。

Main slogan:

> 你今日 fo 了 mo？

## Slide 1 - Cover

Title: Time Slice Coffee

Core line: 你今日 fo 了 mo？

Subtitle: 把今天真正重要的一则变化，装进一杯咖啡里。

Visual: Coffee cup with a data label, pickup-style brand moment.

Claim: This is not another AI SaaS; it is an information consumer product for the AI age.

## Slide 2 - Problem

Title: Every Signal Pretends to Matter

Chinese title: 每一条信息都装作自己很重要

Core line: AI 时代的问题不是信息不够，而是每天都有太多数据信号在制造 FOMO。

Visual: One person surrounded by AI updates, market signals, code snippets, and social feeds.

Claim: People are not just overwhelmed by content; they are pressured by signals pretending to be urgent.

## Slide 3 - Insight

Title: Understanding Becomes a Product

Chinese title: 理解，正在变成一种消费品

Core line:

过去，人们买水是为了解渴。
后来，人们购买品牌、身份和生活方式。
而 AI 时代，人们开始购买对这个时代的理解。

Visual: Water to branded drink to lifestyle coffee to information coffee.

Claim: The product is built on a consumption shift from content to packaged understanding.

## Slide 4 - Product

Title: An Information Coffee Bar for the AI Age

Chinese title: AI 时代的信息咖啡吧

Core line: Time Slice Coffee 把真实信息流中的重要变化，萃取成不同身份可理解、可带走的时代信息切片咖啡。

Visual: Coffee menu with 6 information shelves, persona, density, and served mode.

Claim: Reading information becomes ordering a cup.

## Slide 5 - Workflow

Title: From Raw Signal to Brew Result

Chinese title: 从原始信号到一杯信息咖啡

Core flow: Raw Signal -> Extracted Slice -> Persona -> Density -> Served Mode -> Brew Result -> Label

Visual: Horizontal coffee-making process using beans, extraction, lens, strength, hot/iced, brew, and takeaway label metaphors.

Claim: The value is not summarization; it is extraction, identity translation, concentration control, and consumer packaging.

## Slide 6 - Demo

Title: One Signal, Six Different Values

Chinese title: 同一条信号，六种不同价值

Core line: 同一条 Raw Signal，对 Student、Builder、Designer、Founder、VC、Everyday Person 会产生完全不同的意义。

Visual: One raw signal branching into six labeled cups.

Claim: The same change becomes different personal meanings for different identities.

## Slide 7 - Technology

Title: Stable Today, Real-Time Tomorrow

Chinese title: 今天稳定演示，未来实时接入

Core line: 当前采用预置 xAI / Grok signal bank 保证现场演示稳定，未来可接入实时 Grok / X API。

Visual: Two-column plan: stable demo today and real-time future tomorrow.

Claim: The demo is stable now, while the architecture points toward real-time information coffee menus.

## Slide 8 - Closing

Title: Don’t Read the Age. Drink It.

Chinese title: 不要只是阅读时代，把它喝下去

Core line: Time Slice Coffee 让人们不只是刷到信息，而是点单、萃取、理解，并带走一杯属于自己的时代切片。

Visual: Final pickup page and coffee cup label close-up.

Closing line: 你今日 fo 了 mo？
