# Time Slice Coffee Design Review Notes

## What Improved

- Strengthened the Time Slice Label from a decorative sticker into the visible product system.
- Reused a consistent label schema across Cover, Product, Workflow, Demo, and Closing.
- Made the core framework visible: Raw Signal -> Extracted Slice -> Persona -> Density -> Served Mode -> Brew Result -> Label / Pick Up.
- Reworked Slide 6 so the product differentiation is clearer: one Raw Signal now becomes six persona-specific mini labels.

## Slides Refined

- Slide 1: Made “你今日 fo 了 mo？” more prominent and upgraded the cover label with Shelf, Persona, Density, Served, and Today’s Slice fields.
- Slide 3: Strengthened the evolution from Water / Brand / Coffee to Info Coffee as a new category.
- Slide 4: Replaced generic order layers with a receipt-style ordering system.
- Slide 5: Added visible Persona, Density, and Served Mode steps inside the workflow.
- Slide 6: Rebuilt the demo visual around one central Raw Signal and six persona output labels.
- Slide 7: Simplified technology into Plan B stable demo today and Plan A real-time tomorrow.
- Slide 8: Added a full Pick Up Label schema and reinforced the closing product loop.

## Label System Connection

The label now expresses the product framework directly. It carries the shelf, raw signal, extracted slice, persona, density, served mode, brew result, and takeaway. This makes the deck feel more like a coffee brand launching an information consumer product, rather than a generic AI tool demo.

## Remaining Limitations

- Generated coffee visuals remain concept mockups, not real product photography.
- The label printing module is still presented as future work, not a stable demo feature.
- Some small label fields are intentionally compact to preserve the premium packaging feel on 16:9 slides.

## Final Visual QA Pass

- Brand feel: still reads as a warm minimal coffee brand with a sharp data-label system, not a generic AI SaaS deck.
- Label consistency: Cover, Workflow, Demo, and Closing now share the same Time Slice label grammar: espresso header, schema fields, barcode marks, and roast-red accent.
- Slide 5: full workflow remains visible from Raw Signal to Label, with Persona, Density, and Served Mode called out as intermediate product layers.
- Slide 6: clearly shows one central Raw Signal becoming six persona-specific output labels.
- Slide 8: closes as a Pick Up / Label moment, with the product loop visible beside the final cup imagery.
- Layout QA: rendered all 8 slides and checked for clipped text, overcrowding, margins, typography consistency, and label-schema continuity. Final micro-adjustments improved Slide 5 support-line contrast and Slide 6 mini-label readability.
